# Privacy Policy for Text to Speech (T 2 S)

**Last updated:** August 28, 2026  
**App name:** Text to Speech (T 2 S)  
**Developer:** John Colani  
**Package / App ID:** com.johnacolani.text_to_speech

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Text to Speech app ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our application on your device. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

---

## 2. Data We Process

### 2.1 Text and content you provide

- **Text you type or paste** into the app is used only to generate speech (text-to-speech) on your device.
- **Files you select** (e.g. TXT, PDF, DOC) are read and processed on your device to extract text for reading aloud.
- **Images you select or capture** (camera or photo library) are processed on your device to extract text (OCR) so it can be read aloud. We do not upload your images to our servers.

### 2.2 Local history and settings

- The app may store **history** of text you have had read aloud (including optional saved audio file paths), and your **settings** (e.g. language, voice, speed, pitch) on your device. This data stays in your device’s app storage and is not sent to our servers.

### 2.3 Generated audio

- Speech is generated using your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine. Generated audio may be saved **only on your device** (e.g. in the app’s TTS_audio folder) for playback, including when you are offline. We do not collect or store this audio on our servers.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

- To provide **text-to-speech** and **playback** of the content you choose.
- To **extract text** from files and images you select, so that text can be read aloud.
- To **save history and preferences** locally so you can reuse them within the app.
- We do **not** use your content for advertising, analytics, or selling to third parties.

---

## 4. Where Your Data Is Stored

- All text, file content, images, and generated audio that you use in the app are processed and stored **on your device** (in the app’s private storage or in locations you choose, e.g. when saving files).
- We do **not** transmit your typed text, uploaded files, or photos to our servers for processing. Text-to-speech and OCR (where used) are performed on your device or by system/on-device services.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

- **Google ML Kit** (text recognition / OCR): When you use image-based text extraction, processing may use Google’s on-device ML Kit. Please refer to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how Google handles data in their services.
- **Device TTS engines**: Speech is generated using your operating system’s text-to-speech (e.g. Apple or Android system TTS). Their use of data is governed by the respective platform’s policies.
- We do not control third-party services; we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

---

## 6. Permissions We Request

We request only the permissions needed for the features you use:

| Permission   | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| **Microphone** | Used by the system for text-to-speech output where required by the platform. |
| **Camera**     | To take photos of documents or text so the app can extract and read the text aloud. |
| **Photo library** | To choose images from your device for text extraction (OCR) and reading aloud. |
| **Storage / Files** | To open files (e.g. TXT, PDF) you select and to save generated audio on your device. |

You can revoke these permissions in your device settings at any time. Some features may stop working if you do.

---

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

- Data stored by the app (history, settings, saved audio paths) remains on your device until you **uninstall the app** or **clear the app’s data** in your device settings.
- We do not retain copies of your data on our servers.

---

## 8. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, please contact us and we will work to remove it from the device and our records to the extent possible.

---
